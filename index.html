Cuiwen (Frank) Zhu

My name is Cuiwen (Frank) Zhu. I am a master’s student in the Department of Combinatorics and Optimization at the University of Waterloo, under the supervision of Chris Godsil.

I am interested in algebraic graph theory and algebraic combinatorics, and related problems motivated by quantum computing.
